    About

    I study Information Systems in the Advanced Program at UIT, VNU-HCM, where I was ranked first in my cohort.

    Most of my work sits behind the interface: NestJS services, PostgreSQL schemas, authentication that rotates its own tokens. On the AI side I build retrieval pipelines that cite what they read and reinforcement learning agents that have to earn their reward.

    I like problems where the obvious solution is easy to cheat, such as a check-in that a screenshot cannot fake or a medical answer that cannot be made up.

    Figure 2 · Nov 2025–Feb 2026

    Diabetes Q&A (RAG)

    • Retrieval-augmented generation to stop LLM hallucinations in a medical domain: answers come with the passages they rely on.
    • Data pipeline: BeautifulSoup crawler, custom cleaning for medical PDF and HTML, semantic chunking.
    • Vector embeddings in ChromaDB for fast similarity search over the knowledge base.
    • Local inference through Ollama: no external API ever sees a patient's question.

    Figure 3 · 2026

    RL Dynamic Pricing

    • An agent that sets product prices in real time from demand and competitor signals.
    • The store is modelled as a Markov decision process with a reward that weighs today's profit against customer retention.
    • Demand forecasting in NumPy and Pandas feeds the agent its state.
    • Simulations show the agent converging on a better strategy than static pricing.

    Source code on GitHub
