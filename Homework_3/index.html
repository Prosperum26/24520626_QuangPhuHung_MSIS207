Why the ocean needs us

Healthy seas depend on everyday choices. In this workshop we turn good intentions into small, repeatable actions.

    Spot the plastics you use every day and swap them for lasting alternatives.

  • Protect coastal habitats

    See how reefs, mangroves and beaches shelter marine life, and how to leave them undisturbed.

  • Act together locally

    Find clean-ups and community groups near you, and plan your first step.
